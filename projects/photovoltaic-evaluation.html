Energy & Sustainability

Photovoltaic Technology Impact Evaluation

Sustainability AnalystJan 2025 — May 2025

Project overview

From question to tested outcome.

A comparative assessment of photovoltaic materials through the lenses of performance, economics, carbon impact, and commercial readiness.

The study compared monocrystalline silicon, cadmium telluride, and emerging perovskite cells, connecting material structure and bandgap to system-level adoption.

Key work & outcomes

  • Quantified levelized cost of electricity and life-cycle carbon footprint.
  • Validated model-generated technical claims against peer-reviewed literature.
  • Connected material properties to performance and commercialization constraints.
