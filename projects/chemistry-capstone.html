12-Tungstosilicic Acid Synthesis

Capstone ResearcherJan 2025 — May 2025

Project overview

From question to tested outcome.

A chemistry capstone investigating the synthesis, characterization, and catalytic behavior of 12-tungstosilicic acid.

Literature-guided synthesis was paired with UV-Vis, infrared spectroscopy, and proton NMR to evaluate product identity and catalytic performance.

Key work & outcomes

  • Designed a literature-informed synthesis and characterization workflow.
  • Used spectroscopy to identify impurities and explain catalytic ineffectiveness.
  • Proposed solvent extraction and further optimization pathways.
