Testing & Materials

Material Properties of Steel & Concrete

Materials Testing EngineerMay 2026

Project overview

From question to tested outcome.

Laboratory characterization of steel and concrete under tension and compression using standardized test methods and full stress-strain analysis.

Steel coupons and 35-day-aged concrete cylinders were tested with high-capacity universal testing equipment under AS1391 and AS1012.9.

Key work & outcomes

  • Operated 300 kN Sintech and 2000 kN DARTEC test machines.
  • Calculated Young’s modulus, yield stress, ultimate strength, and ductility.
  • Interpreted stress-strain curves to characterize failure behavior.
