2.5 Degree of Freedom Cartesian Motor System

Electromechanical DesignerSept 2026 — Present

Project Overview

A six week design project to design build and test a compact 2.5 Degree of Freedom (DoF) Cartesian System with mechanical, electrical, and control systems.

An in-progress Cartesian motion platform integrating mechanical design, actuation, and controls into a coordinated electromechanical system.

The project focuses on translating positional requirements into a stable physical architecture, with iterative CAD, component selection, assembly, and testing.

Key Work & Outcomes

  • Developed a modular 2.5 DoF Cartesian system architecture.
  • Integrated mechanical and electrical subsystems around motion requirements.
  • Established an iterative workflow for prototyping and validation.
