2.5 Degree of Freedom Cartesian Motor System

Electromechanical DesignerSept 2026 — Present

Project overview

A backpack-portable 2.5-DoF Cartesian motion system.

In a six-week project, I am designing, building, and testing a compact Cartesian motion system that combines mechanical, electrical, and control design.

The system must cover at least a 2.5 in × 2.5 in work area, pack into a backpack, and be assembled by one person in under 10 minutes. Aluminum extrusions (20 mm or 25 mm) act as both the frame and the linear guides to cut down on machining, with 3D-printed and laser-cut mounts, stepper motors, timing belts, and pulleys, plus a custom end-effector for a chosen application.

The work moves from sketches and CAD through linear-stage simulation, assembly, and electronics integration to testing for accuracy and stability.

Key work & outcomes

  • Developed a modular 2.5-DoF Cartesian architecture.
  • Built a prototype linear stage driven by G-code.
  • Drew a square with two linear axes as a first motion test.
  • Set up an iterative prototyping and testing workflow.
