Steel C-Channel Beam Deflection Analysis

Structural AnalystMar 2026

Project overview

From question to tested outcome.

An experimental and analytical study of a simply supported steel C-channel subjected to asymmetric incremental loading.

Dial-gauge measurements were compared with derived deflection and bending-moment expressions to test how closely theory predicted physical behavior.

Key work & outcomes

  • Measured deflection under incremental loads up to 222.5 N.
  • Derived analytical deflection and bending-moment expressions.
  • Validated experimental results within 10% of theoretical predictions.
