Optimized Acrylic Truss Design

Mechanical Design LeadFeb 2025 — May 2025

Project overview

From question to tested outcome.

An eight-joint acrylic Warren truss optimized for high load capacity, predictable failure behavior, and material-efficient fabrication.

The design paired analytical structural mechanics with a custom MATLAB model and physical fabrication to predict performance before testing.

Key work & outcomes

  • Reached a predicted failure load of 65 oz.
  • Calculated member axial forces and buckling thresholds in MATLAB.
  • Used nominal, strong, and weak cases to quantify uncertainty.
