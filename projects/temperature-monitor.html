Arduino Temperature Sensor Monitor

Engineering StudentJan 2025 — May 2025

Project overview

From question to tested outcome.

This project details the design and prototyping of a room temperature monitoring system built to track environmental temperatures and provide real-time alerts.

Controlled by an Arduino Uno, the system continuously reads data from a TMP36 temperature sensor and displays the current temperature on a 16x2 alphanumeric I2C LCD. Whenever the temperature falls outside the comfortable operating range of 0°C to 100°C, the system triggers a flashing red LED and a Piezo buzzer to immediately alert the user.

Key work & outcomes

  • Engineered a compact, functional prototype housed in an ABS enclosure using an Arduino Uno to process analog voltage signals into discrete data for the LCD and sensor.
  • Designed a dual-alert system featuring a green LED power indicator and a high-visibility flashing red LED paired with a Piezo buzzer for out-of-range temperature warnings.
  • Analyzed power consumption, determining an operating time of approximately 35.56 hours using a 9V, 500 mAh battery drawing 14.06 mA of current.
  • Documented the complete design process, material selection, circuit wiring, and system evaluation in the EK131 Final Report.
