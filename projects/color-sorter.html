Automated Color Sorter

Engineering DesignerJun 2026 — Aug 2026

Project overview

From question to tested outcome.

A compact automated device that detects five color channels and mechanically sorts objects into transparent collection bins.

The 2-by-2-foot system combines microcontroller-based sensing, hands-free feeding, and electromechanical actuation in a build constrained by cost and throughput.

Key work & outcomes

  • Reliably sorted 21 objects per minute.
  • Designed a clean, hands-free feeding and multi-bin collection system.
  • Kept the complete prototype within a $200 budget.
